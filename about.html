About

QR Code Generator is a free collection of QR code tools: a general QR code generator, plus generators for Wi-Fi, contacts, email and SMS. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and the vendored qrcode-generator library. There is no account, no sign-up and no server: nothing you type is sent anywhere, stored or logged.

QR codes are free to generate and free to use. Our tools add no watermark and impose no scan limits.

Contact us at contact@qrcodegenerator.github.io.
